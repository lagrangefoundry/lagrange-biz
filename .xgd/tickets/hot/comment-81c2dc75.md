## What was not achieved, and it should not be quietly folded in

This goal claimed two things. The first was onboarding. The second was **rehearsal** - *"a rehearsal for both the wider beta and the class. n=1 in-house costs almost nothing and de-risks both."*

The rehearsal did not happen. He has not had time to use it, so there is no usage, no bug reports from a fresh pair of eyes, and no evidence about what a newcomer finds confusing. The de-risking this goal promised to the beta (goal-af871b76) and to the class cohort (goal-428b7163) has not been delivered.

Recording that plainly because both of those goals lean on it. Anyone reading `realized` here and concluding the onboarding path is validated would be drawing more from it than happened: **it is proven installable, not proven usable.**

The practical consequence is for xgd/GOAL-12 (documentation) and goal-adac9e3a (interface tidy-up) - the questions n=1 was going to answer cheaply are still open, and the next chance to answer them is the class cohort, where the stakes are higher and the audience is not in the house.

No blame in this. Summer breaks end and teenagers have better things to do. But an opportunity taken and an opportunity realised are different, and the map should say which this was.
