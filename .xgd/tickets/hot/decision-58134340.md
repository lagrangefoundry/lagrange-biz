Introduce `goal` and `decision` ticket types and a goal map, with cross-project federation (DOC-19, DOC-14).

**Consequences.** lagrange-biz becomes the master planning project holding the major business goals, with product and technical goals federated in as children living in their own projects. Business and marketing goals belong here; technical and product goals belong to xgd, 1stcontact and lagrange-framework.

**The rule that decides placement:** a goal belongs to the project that can complete it.
