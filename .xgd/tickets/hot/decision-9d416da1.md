Four linked calls made together:

1. **Take the class window.** Hard expiry end of August. First external user cohort of anything; validates 1c and rehearses the education channel. Accepted as an explicit trade against XGD beta timing.
2. **Start beta at n=1 immediately** — my son, on summer break, within two weeks. Requires a stable release and basic packaging with an update path, which become the key short-term XGD goals. Everything else goes to 1c.
3. **Reframe beta as a learning instrument, not a launch.** The entry bar becomes support capacity, not defect rate. XGD beta remains the goal; rushing it would be a mistake, and so would waiting for quality to be 'done'.
4. **Postpone the modular editor showcase** — postponed, not retired. 1stcontact is the better demonstration because it is a product.

**Correction recorded:** we are past the worst absolutely-blocking quality issues, which is *not* the same as being past the quality problem.
