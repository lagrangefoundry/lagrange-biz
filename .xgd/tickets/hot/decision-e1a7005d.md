Rather than building a website for XGD, build a website-building tool first, then use it to build the XGD site.

**Date from evidence:** first commit in the 1stcontact repository, 2026-06-29.

**Consequences.** This is the single largest reshaping of the plan in this period. It created an entire product (1stcontact) with its own market, revenue stream and roadmap, and it pushed XGD closed beta out by months. It is also what made the class-cohort opportunity possible at all.

**Not a planning failure.** It traded schedule for assets, and the assets are real: a product, a design vocabulary, and the strongest available proof that XGD builds serious software.
