## Shape of the change

This is not a deployment tidy-up. "proxy deleted", "control-app becomes the builder", "the AI host and publish move into workerd" are a change to what the system *is*. The filesystem SiteStore becomes an async port with D1 and R2 behind it; render moves to the edge; the builder becomes a private authenticated app rather than something fronted by a proxy.

DOC-5 already specified a Cloudflare-first architecture - Workers, D1, R2, KV, Durable Objects, Queues. This is that specification being made real rather than a new direction.
