Bugs in the resync workflow - rebasing the working branch tail onto an updated main - worked on 2026-08-06 and 2026-08-07.

Locking and index contention stalled resync twice: BUG-937 (remove_index_entry self-deadlocks inside an active index_batch, stalling both resync and reconcile) and BUG-942 (hot-index lock contention causes a remap_commits terminal failure on 1stcontact).

Conflict resolution was the other theme: BUG-953 (the cherry-pick window does not detect revert-pairs, so it burns an LLM turn and terminal-fails on net-zero commits) and BUG-954 (single-shot conflict resolution with no retry, unlike reconcile - the BUG-773 gap).

BUG-939 was a lagrange-framework resync failure, closed as a duplicate of BUG-938.

Worth noting: resync and reconcile share the cherry-pick machinery, so several bugs filed under one workflow bit in the other. BUG-937 is filed here but stalled both.
