The bar for letting people in.

**The reframe:** beta's purpose is to accelerate learning, not to earn revenue or demonstrate polish. Quality is *not* fixed — we are past the worst absolutely-blocking issues, which is not the same thing. Under a quality-gated framing those two facts conflict and beta waits indefinitely. Under a learning framing they do not: the bar becomes *"can I support a handful of friendly users through failures without it consuming me"* rather than *"is the defect rate low enough"*.

That implies a **smaller, earlier, higher-touch** beta than originally planned, and it moves the gating item from defect count to install path plus support capacity.
