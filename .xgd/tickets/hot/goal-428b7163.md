The first external user cohort of anything the operator has built. It answers the one open question no amount of internal work can - does the AI produce something a real user would actually publish. It also rehearses education-channel delivery (a segment in the business plan) and generates content and testimonials as a by-product.

**What it is not:** XGD validation. They are 1c users, not xgd users. This buys product learning and channel rehearsal, not beta references.

### What is actually left on milestone 1

Not volume. Two specific things and one absence:

- **BUG-48** - *a document in the corpus that is not in the index is a shipped lie* - draft. This is the risk REQ-158 predicted on 08-28 when it recorded that 1c kb build had never been run end to end against the real corpus. It has now surfaced as a real defect. A KMS that silently cannot see part of its own corpus is worse than one that is visibly incomplete, because the demo would look like it worked.
- **REQ-171** - review the session prompts and turn reminders, make the chat summary one of them - draft. This is the priming-quality pass, which is what (a) actually asks for.
- **(c) demo sites** - still has no representation anywhere. It is the only requirement whose done state is judged by eye rather than by tests, and it is the part the cohort actually sees. Three days out, it remains the least-defended item on this goal.

## The deadline that moved

Worth stating plainly rather than burying: this is the second target on this goal. The original was set by decision-9d416da1 on 2026-08-06 as a hard external window - *if 1c is not usable by then they will use something else and the window closes*. It was missed, and the reason is legible rather than mysterious: the platform migration to Cloudflare (realized 08-24) and the access-control refactor (realized 08-19) both landed in between, and both were prerequisites rather than detours.

What that bought, measured against this goal: an app that runs in the cloud where a cohort can reach it, a KMS the assistant primes from, an upload path with a Library the client can read, and an identity model that lets more than one person log in. None of those existed on 08-06 when the original date was set.
