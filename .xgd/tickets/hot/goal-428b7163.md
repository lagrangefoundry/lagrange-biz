The first external user cohort of anything the operator has built. It answers the one open question no amount of internal work can - does the AI produce something a real user would actually publish. It also rehearses education-channel delivery (a segment in the business plan) and generates content and testimonials as a by-product.

**What it is not:** XGD validation. They are 1c users, not xgd users. This buys product learning and channel rehearsal, not beta references.

## Read on the shape of this

Five days to milestone 1, and **everything under it is `draft` or `concept`** - nothing is in flight. The KMS block alone is 8 + 13 + 8 = 29 story points across REQ-158, REQ-159 and REQ-160, and REQ-158 records that `1c kb build` has never been run end to end against the real corpus, so the embed, describe and awareness passes are unexercised.

That is not a prediction about whether it lands - points are coarse size and this map does not do velocity. It is an observation that the demo date has no work started against it, and that (c) demo sites, the part a viewer actually sees, has no representation anywhere at all.

**Milestone 2 depends on milestone 1 in practice** - there is no point opening trials on a system that cannot be demonstrated - but the two are also the classic split: (1) is capability, (2) is access. They fail differently and should be tracked apart.

## The deadline that moved

Worth stating plainly rather than burying: this is the second target on this goal. The original was set by decision-9d416da1 on 2026-08-06 as a hard external window. It was missed, and the reason is legible in the record rather than mysterious - the platform migration to Cloudflare (realized 08-24) and the access-control rewrite (realized 08-19) both landed in that window, and both were chosen deliberately over shipping on the laptop.

That is a trade, not a slip. But the cohort window was described as non-recoverable, and it has now been recovered once by rescheduling - which is worth knowing about the next time something is called a hard external deadline.
