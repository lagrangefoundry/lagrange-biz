XGD works dependably enough that someone other than its author can run it.

A root rather than a child of XGD closed beta. Composition is the test: reliability is not *part of* the beta - the beta, the paid launch and the built-with-itself proof story all rest on it. Putting it under any one of them would hide it from the other two.

**Composition:**

- XGD Remove friction - the operator-ergonomics burst of 2026-08-07.
- Reconcile / Resync / Development / Regression bugs - the four workflow stages.
- Ticketing store bugs - a fifth cluster, split out because the root cause is the store rather than any one workflow.
- Dashboard and goal map bugs - including this interface.
- ticket://lagrangefoundry/xgd/GOAL-1 - Stable release: regression green end-to-end.
- ticket://lagrangefoundry/xgd/GOAL-2 - Packaging with an update path.

**Ordering not yet expressed.** XGD closed beta (goal-af871b76) and First external user onboarded (goal-1789a4b5, target 2026-08-20) both arguably depend_on this goal: onboarding an external user onto a platform whose reconcile stalls mid-bundle is how a single expiring opportunity gets spent badly. That edge has deliberately not been added - it would pull the beta off the ready frontier, which is a call for the operator rather than for me.

**Scale, for the record.** Forty-five tickets over 2026-08-06 and 2026-08-07: 31 bugs (BUG-933 to BUG-971, with gaps) and 14 requests (REQ-743 to REQ-758). Of the 31 bugs: 10 reached free_coded, 11 ready_to_reconcile, 6 legacy_done, 2 abandoned as duplicates, 1 wont_fix, 1 still draft. That is two days.

**Known limitation.** Roll-up derivation follows only goals resolvable in this store, so cross-project children (ticket:// refs into xgd and 1stcontact) contribute nothing to a parent derived state. Any goal here whose real content lives in another project will under-derive permanently. This accounts for three of the four standing disagreements on the map.
