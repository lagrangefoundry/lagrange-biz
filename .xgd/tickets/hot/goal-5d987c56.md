## Scope, as stated by the operator

Two distinct classes, and the second is the one that is easy to lose behind the first:

1. **Display problems** - the matrix does not render correctly.
2. **Bugs in the capability matrix system itself** - it is not working perfectly. These are correctness defects in how capabilities are derived, recorded or maintained, not presentation.

The goal is elimination of class 2, not merely a matrix that looks right. A matrix that displays cleanly but derives wrongly is worse than one that visibly fails, because it is trusted.

## Why it matters beyond tidiness

The capability matrix is the accumulation register. It is the artefact that answers "what do I have now that I did not have in May", and it is the evidence base for the accountability claim in DOC-4 and DOC-8 - that trust in AI-written software must be structural rather than promised. A matrix with known defects cannot carry that argument, and it cannot be handed to a beta user as proof of anything.
