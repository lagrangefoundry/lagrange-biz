XGD sessions get the context that is actually relevant to what they are doing.

**The completion of the backend refactor.** Operator intent, 2026-08-13: once the access control work is stable, introducing the knowledge management system finishes the job started by adopting lagrange.ai as the AI backend.

## The payoff, in the operator words

"This opens the door to make the XGD sessions have much more relevant context and do fewer stupid things requiring my constant redirection."

Worth stating plainly because it is the clearest statement of value in the map: the cost being paid today is **the operator attention**, spent redirecting sessions that lack the context to do the right thing unprompted. That is a tax on every thread, every day, and it is invisible in any ticket count.

It also compounds with everything else. The operator runs six to nine threads across three projects; a session that needs less steering is not a small efficiency, it is the difference between running those threads and being run by them.

## Sequencing

Depends on goal-959f56f3 (LLM access control refactored onto the lagrange.ai backend). Deliberately after, not alongside - the backend adoption has to be stable before another major subsystem lands on it.
