Chat and Docs become a single Design tab, matching how the work is actually done rather than how the features were built.

Scope discussion opened in a chat session on 2026-08-11.

The rationale is the operator own development workflow: a feature starts as a high-level chat about requirements and architecture, becomes a design document, and then becomes REQ tickets. Chat and Docs are two halves of one activity, presented as two tabs because they were built as two features. A newcomer has no way to infer that the conversation and the document belong together.

No tickets yet - scope is still being worked out.
