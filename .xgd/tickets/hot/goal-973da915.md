**Standing goal - ongoing, not a goal that completes.** Remove the operator-facing friction in running XGD day to day: the small costs that are individually tolerable and collectively decide whether an external user survives their first week. Work lands here continuously; it is a home for friction as it is found, not a deliverable with a finish line.

Opened as a burst on the afternoon of 2026-08-07 (REQ-749 through REQ-757, roughly 15:10-16:50 local), plus REQ-758 the following morning. Three themes:

- **Branch and process lifecycle** - REQ-749 (parallel branch lifecycle: xgd branch cut/lock/clean), REQ-752 (dispatcher daemon bare-restart + dashboard restart commands), REQ-758 (dashboard restart rebinds to the previously running port).
- **Operator observability** - REQ-750 (file a bug in xgd straight from a process log; xgd ticket create --project), REQ-753 (Hard Revert / Delete Tags / Restart buttons on the Logs tab tag list).
- **Packaging and release** - REQ-754 (xgd update command), REQ-755 (./bin/build release-packaging), REQ-756 (./bin/deploy release-publishing).

Note on overlap: the packaging trio duplicates ticket://lagrangefoundry/xgd/GOAL-2 (Packaging with an update path, planned). They are listed here because they were opened as friction work, but the packaging goal is the better long-term owner.

Provenance is discovered rather than planned: none of these were on a roadmap. They were found by using the system hard enough to feel where it rubs.
