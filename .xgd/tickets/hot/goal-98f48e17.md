This interface answers what should I work on next well enough to be used every morning without effort.

Operator assessment on 2026-08-08: struggling with the UX. Three defects are known and specific.

## 1. The ready frontier is dominated by non-actionable goals

The frontier returns 17 items. **Two of them are actually actionable.** Everything else is a root or a branch - XGD quality, Web presence, Proof and demonstration, Positioning and narrative, XGD closed beta, and the six workflow bug goals. You cannot sit down and do Web presence.

The readiness rule is state in (planned, in_progress) AND every depends_on realized. It has no notion of altitude, so a root that is in_progress by roll-up rule 3 is as ready as a leaf.

Two changes, and both are needed:

- **Exclude goals that have live children.** A parent is defined by its children, so the work is in the children, not the parent. Only leaves are actionable.
- **Count cross-project children as children even when unresolvable.** Today they do not resolve locally, so every cross-project parent - Reconcile bugs, Remove friction, First external user, 1stcontact as public proof - looks like a leaf and floods the frontier. This is the same root cause as defect 3.

Applied together these cut 17 to a handful.

## 2. No way to express a standing goal

The state ladder assumes goals terminate. XGD Remove friction and the four workflow bug goals never complete - they are containers that accept work indefinitely. They can never reach realized, so they contribute nothing to the accumulation register and sit on the frontier forever. Five of the seven children of XGD quality are in this position.

## 3. Roll-up ignores cross-project children

Derivation follows only goals resolvable in the local store. Two realized 1stcontact goals (Draft site, Draft site served from Cloudflare) were wired under XGD site live on 2026-08-08 and did not move its derived state at all. Any goal whose real content lives in another project under-derives permanently, which manufactures false staleness signals - three of the four standing disagreements on this map are this, not drift.
