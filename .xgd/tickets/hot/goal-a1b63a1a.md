XGD is something a person other than its author can be handed without a guided tour.

Distinct from XGD quality, which is about the system working correctly, and from XGD Remove friction, which is about the operator own speed. This goal is about **legibility and trust for a newcomer** - two properties that only become visible when you picture someone else at the keyboard.

**Composition:**

- AI access control is transparent (goal-959f56f3) - can they see what the agent is allowed to do.
- Interface tidy-up for external use (goal-adac9e3a) - can they work out what a control does.

## Origin

Lunch with the n=1 external user on 2026-08-10. Eight requests followed on 2026-08-11. See the decision record for why this is user research rather than a detour.

## Why it sits under XGD closed beta

The beta is the first time XGD is used by people who did not build it. Everything here is a precondition for that going well rather than a nice-to-have afterwards, and the n=1 onboarding on 2026-08-20 is the first test.

Worth noting the sequencing this implies: the install tool (goal-d5e96abe) gets someone to a running system, and this goal decides whether what they find is usable once they are there. Packaging without legibility produces a successful install and a confused user.
