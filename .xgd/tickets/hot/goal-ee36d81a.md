## The open question is the point of this goal

An undifferentiated refactoring backlog in front of a dated beta is the classic way a launch slips without anyone deciding it should. Nothing here is wrong; what is missing is the line between must-happen-first and can-follow.

Until that line is drawn, this goal cannot be sequenced against XGD closed beta, and the beta has no honest readiness signal - which means the map cannot answer "are we ready" for the one commitment it most needs to.

**Suggested next step:** walk the backlog once and mark each item blocking or not. That is a short exercise and it converts a vague weight into a scoped dependency.
